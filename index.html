Cross-Department

Inter-Department Workflow & Rules

We run on a system, not on favours.

Before — No System

  • Direct WhatsApp tags to designers/copywriters
  • Private msgs: "really urgent please help"
  • 5-day lead time ignored, always last minute
  • Poster in group "pls check" = expected QC
  • No data ownership → mistakes blamed on marketing
  • Think of idea → immediately want poster/video
  • No tracking, tasks fall through cracks

After — Systematic

  • All requests via ClickUp Creative Request Form
  • Auto-tracked task with brief + deadline
  • 5 working days enforced — emergency needs Manager OK
  • Deliver through ClickUp with documentation
  • Data accuracy = requester's responsibility always
  • Max 2 revision rounds per request
  • Full audit trail: request→assign→deliver

Creative Request Workflow

1

Dept Fills ClickUp Creative Request Form

Must include: what, purpose, audience, all data/info, reference, deadline (min 5 working days). No form = no work.

2

Auto-Created Task → Content Lead / Manager Assigns

Prioritized by workload, assigned to right person. Requester sees status in ClickUp.

3

Work in Progress (within lead time)

All updates in ClickUp comments. Not WhatsApp.

4

Internal QC

Self-check → Lead review → DL grammar. We check: visual quality, brand, language, formatting.

5

Requester Verifies Data

Names, dates, numbers, transaction values, rankings — their responsibility. Post-publish data errors are on the requesting department.

6

Approved → Deliver / Publish

Delivered through ClickUp. Social content follows posting SOP.

The 6 Rules

ClickUp Form Only
No form = no work. No WhatsApp, no verbal, no "just quickly."
5-Day Lead Time
Minimum. Emergency = Manager approval + subject to workload.
Data = Requester's Job
We check language & brand. Factual accuracy is theirs.
Max 2 Revisions
Beyond 2 rounds = new request. Clear briefs upfront.
No Direct Assignment
Submit the form. We assign internally by workload.
Deliver via ClickUp
WhatsApp group ≠ delivery. ClickUp with docs and sign-off.

Separate Proposal — Not Under Marketing Department

Recruitment is not part of the Branding & Marketing team. However, because marketing controls the ad pipeline generating recruitment leads, and the previous handler is leaving with no documented system, I am proposing to take over restructuring this function. This is a strategic decision for company growth — the investment in ads must actually convert to hires. I will oversee the system design and team setup while keeping it operationally independent from marketing.

Separate Proposal

Recruitment Pipeline Restructuring

From "run ads and hope" to a system with tracking at every stage.

Current Problems

No Documented Flow

Leads come in → one person contacts them → messy follow-up → no tracking → if she's absent, pipeline is invisible. She is now leaving.

No Conversion Tracking

400+ leads but no data on: CPL, response rate, interview rate, conversion rate, time-to-close, which campaigns perform. Money spent blind.

No Nurturing

Leads who say "not now" are lost. No follow-up pipeline, no monthly touchpoints. Every lead is one-shot.

Replacement ≠ Solution

Hiring another person just to answer calls doesn't fix the structural problem. Need a system + team with broader scope to justify headcount.

Proposed Funnel

Meta Lead Ads + Engagement Ads (run by Marketing Dept)
Ads, targeting, creative — handled by marketing. Generates top of funnel.
▼
Leads Captured in CRM / Tracking System
Auto-logged: source, date, campaign, status. Every lead visible & accountable.
▼
Recruitment Exec Contacts Within 24 Hours
Call/WhatsApp — qualify interest: Interested / Not Ready / No Response
▼
Interested → Schedule Interview with Leader
CMO assigns to appropriate team leader for interview
▼
Accept → Onboard → Track Full Conversion
Measure: cost-per-hire, conversion rate, time-to-join, best campaigns

Not Ready → Nurture Pipeline

Monthly touchpoint. Share PropNex culture & success content. Re-enter funnel when ready.

No Response → 3-Attempt Protocol

3 attempts over 2 weeks → archive. Quarterly mass re-engagement campaign.
